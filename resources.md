  <h2>Sometimes the hardest part is knowing where to begin.</h2>
  <p>Families are often expected to navigate a maze of services, providers, programs, and community resources during some of life's most stressful moments. Finding the right support can feel overwhelming when you don't know what's available or where to turn.</p>
  <p>La Loba Pathways connects individuals and families with trusted resources related to caregiving, aging, hospice, serious illness, and end-of-life planning. Whether it's locating community programs, support services, equipment, educational materials, or professional referrals, we help people identify the next steps that best fit their needs.</p>
  <p>Our goal is to make the path forward easier to see — and easier to follow.</p>
