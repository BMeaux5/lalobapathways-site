  <h2>Sometimes families need more than information.</h2>
  <h3>Support should not depend on someone's ability to pay.</h3>
  <p>Many individuals and families face financial barriers while navigating caregiving, serious illness, hospice, and end-of-life transitions. Yet these are often the times when support is needed most.</p>
  <p>La Loba Pathways is committed to helping expand access to guidance, resources, and services for those who may not otherwise be able to afford them. Through fundraising efforts, community partnerships, and future assistance programs, we hope to reduce barriers and ensure more people receive the support they deserve.</p>
  <p>Because no one should have to face life's most challenging transitions alone simply because of cost.</p>
  <p>When practical needs arise, La Loba Pathways works to connect individuals and families with funding, donated supplies, and community support.</p>

  <h3>Potential assistance:</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Incontinence supplies</li>
    <li>Personal care items</li>
    <li>Respite support</li>
    <li>Transportation assistance</li>
    <li>Caregiving supplies</li>
    <li>End-of-life comfort items</li>
    <li>Emergency assistance</li>
  </ul>
