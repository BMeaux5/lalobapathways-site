<div class="section">
  <h2>Sometimes people need more than information — they need someone beside them.</h2>
  <p>Healthcare, caregiving, hospice, and aging-related decisions can be overwhelming. Appointments are often rushed, information is complex, and families may feel uncertain about what questions to ask or what options are available.</p>
  <p>La Loba Pathways provides advocacy and support to help individuals and families navigate these conversations with greater confidence.</p>
  <h3>Depending on a family's needs, advocacy support may include:</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Preparing for medical appointments</li>
    <li>Identifying questions and concerns</li>
    <li>Attending meetings or appointments as a support person</li>
    <li>Helping interpret information and next steps</li>
    <li>Facilitating communication among family members</li>
    <li>Connecting families with community resources and services</li>
    <li>Ensuring wishes, priorities, and concerns are clearly expressed</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Our role is to help people feel informed, empowered, and supported as they navigate important decisions.</p>
